# About, in Persian: a draft for review

A draft by Claude (24 October 2026) of the About page in Persian, from the owner's own English.
It is not on the site. Once a fluent reader has checked it, it can become a page at /fa/about,
in the About page's design, set right to left, with `lang="fa"` and each version naming the
other (`hreflang`), so that search engines and AI assistants answering in Persian have the
owner's own words to go on.

To be checked especially (marked ⟨?⟩ below):

1. "Tangata Tiriti" is kept in Māori, with a short gloss. Is the gloss right, and is it wanted?
2. "Persian healing lineages": دودمان‌های درمانگر. Or would حکیمان/طبیبان be nearer the owner's meaning?
3. "chanting": خوانش آیینی و آوازی. The owner may have a word in mind, such as ذکر.
4. "Tîr Aho Studio" is left in Latin letters. Does it have a Persian spelling (تیر آهو?) the owner uses?
5. "Acting Editor": سرپرست ویراستاری. Or ویراستار موقت?
6. *Asia Pacific Art Papers* keeps its English title, with a translation in brackets.
