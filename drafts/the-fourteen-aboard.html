The Fifteen Aboard

The fifteen channellers on the boat: who they are, how strong each one is, and where each one sits. The numbers come from the site's code. This sheet is for designers and for Claude Design, so their work keeps the world's scale and manner.

The boat

The viewer is aboard. The hull is never drawn: the picture is what your eye sees from the deck, riding the real waves. Circle area is how many filaments each one's rope carries when drawn, which is how their strength shows.

  • The voyageA pilgrimage from Manawatāwhi (the Three Kings Islands) to Hawaiʻi, leaving at noon on 10 October 2026 and arriving at noon on 1 July 2027, Hawaiʻi time. When wind and current would carry it faster, the weaker ones slow it with deep weaves close to the sea.
  • In viewManulani at the bow and the front row of four. The other eight sit behind your eye along the rails, and Leilehua at the stern, out of the picture. Their ropes come into view as they leave the boat.
  • Day to dayFour sit at the front and take turns as the weather suits them. Laeni is always one of the four, second from the right. They link to combine their strength, but Pardis joins only when the rest of the four are Keel, Mohabat and Laeni; otherwise she keeps her own tress.
  • TogetherThey sit as in the plan: Keel, Pardis, Laeni and Mohabat across the front row, Manulani at the bow, the other eight down the rails and Leilehua at the stern.
  • HandsEach rope leaves its channeller's hands 0.8 to 1.6 m below your eye. At night with no channelling, the boat's electric lamps are the only light near by.

Strength

Each one's true strength, as a percentage of Laeni's, on a logarithmic scale where each step is ten times the last. Laeni is 3.5 times everyone else together. A rope's mass grows with the cube root of strength, so in the picture her rope carries 1,207 filaments against 82 for the lightest.

How they are drawn

Each strand has a warm white heart, with its element's colour only at its flank. The whole picture is graded muted, like Fuji film. These are the colours of the elements as the site shows them now.

AirDusty lavender#9590c0
WaterGrey sea glass#64a0a4
AkashaPale old gold#cba96e
FireGlowing soot, red to cream#c7755a
EarthOlive grey#8f8a6b
LaeniWhite, or a tinted white#f7f6ff
Laeni's lightBlue-violet, almost white#c2bdff
  • Power is scale, not colour.A stronger one has more filaments in a broader rope, each as faint as anyone's. Never brighter or richer.
  • Curves only.Every form is one continuous curve. Nothing jagged, angular or electric, nothing striking down. Turns are slow.
  • They are healers.A pilgrimage: everything they channel is for the well-being of the sea and the air.
  • The weather leads.They answer the live wind, waves and pressure, never a clock.
  • Laeni reads white.Against sky, sea or any other colour, by day and by night. Her filaments carry only her four elements: air, spirit, water and fire.
  • Real physics, real sea.Shapes come from vortex tubes, swell and wind, never emblems, stars or regular braids. Even the extreme looks like a real sea.
  • Nothing painted on the water.Where they change the sea, the water takes the light differently.
  • No outlines.No navy or blue edges along ropes.
