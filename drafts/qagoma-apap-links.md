## 2. Wikipedia: an edit request on the article's Talk page

Wikipedia's conflict-of-interest guideline asks people not to edit articles about themselves. The
usual route is a request on the Talk page
(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk:Nabil_Sabio_Azadi), posted from the owner's account, which
discloses who is asking. Something like:

> {{Edit COI}}
> I am Nabil Sabio Azadi, the subject of this article, so I am requesting this rather than making it.
>
> Please add to the career section:
>
> From September 2025 to March 2026 Azadi was Acting Editor at the Queensland Art Gallery | Gallery
> of Modern Art (QAGOMA), and he edited the gallery's online publication *Asia Pacific Art Papers*.<ref>{{cite web |title=Acknowledgments |website=Asia Pacific Art Papers
> |publisher=Queensland Art Gallery {{!}} Gallery of Modern Art |url=https://apap.qagoma.qld.gov.au/acknowledgements/
> |access-date=24 October 2026}}</ref>
>
> Thank you. ~~~~

A source the gallery publishes about its own staff counts as primary. Editors usually accept it for
a plain fact like this one, but may ask for an independent source as well.
